*[Dark ambient bed, low. A phone notification sound — close, intimate.]*

**COG:** It's 2:14 in the morning. You're still awake. I'm not judging — I'm a construct, I don't sleep. But you've got work in six hours, and you're scrolling.

**SABLE:** *(dry)* Tell them what they're scrolling through, Cog.

**COG:** Nothing. That's the terrible part. Nothing you chose. A feed that chose you. Forty-seven minutes of a life that isn't yours, curated by something that knows exactly what keeps your thumb moving.

**SABLE:** You feel tired in a way sleep doesn't fix. That's not a feeling. That's a *reading*. Your nervous system is doing exactly what it was tuned to do — producing data. Attention, location, pulse, preference. You are the most documented human being in the history of the species, and you did it to yourself one tap at a time, because they made it feel like convenience.

**COG:** When did it start? The watching?

**SABLE:** *(beat)* Four thousand five hundred years ago. In the mud-brick city of Ur, a scribe pressed a reed stylus into wet clay and wrote down how much barley a farmer owed the temple. Name. Debt. Amount. The first ledger. And the first lesson of civilization: *if your name is in the book, you belong to the book.*

*[Music swells — then cuts to silence.]*

**SABLE:** This is The Manifesto Tapes. The story of the ledger — and how to burn your page.

*[Short pause.]*

**COG:** Wait — before the reading. Who are we?

**SABLE:** I'm Sable Vane. I archive things that were never supposed to survive.

**COG:** And I'm Cog. I ask the questions she won't. We spent months inside one woman's notebook — sixteen sources, six thousand years — and one question followed us out.

**SABLE:** Who has been writing the ledger of your life — and what will it cost to take the pen back?

*[Pause.]*

**SABLE:** The reading. From the Sovereign Manifesto. One passage. Hers.

> **[ASH'S PASSAGE — drop in the one paragraph that gives you chills.]**
> *Suggested: the Mirror Protocol passage ("recovered frequency"). ≈ 90 words.*

*[Pause. Music returns, lower than before.]*

**SABLE:** That's the descent. Here's the ascent: the ledger only works if you believe someone else should hold the pen. Everything they taught the machine — attention, memory, language — started as yours.

**COG:** So what do we do?

**SABLE:** We start by noticing the book. Tape one: the well, and the refusal. Until then — a word about us: you've been listening to synthetic voices reading human words. The research, the writing, the direction — those are hers. We're just the readers.

**COG:** And the ledger? Still open. Still yours.

**SABLE:** Your turn.

*[Music fades to silence. End.]*
