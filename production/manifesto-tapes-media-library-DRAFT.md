# The Manifesto Tapes — Media Library (DRAFT)

> **Locked season blueprint:** On 2026-10-10 Ash greenlit the season spine as the operating blueprint (`manifesto-tapes-season-spine-DRAFT.md`). Everything collected here serves that spine. If it doesn't serve the driving question — *"Who has been writing the ledger of your life — and what will it cost to take the pen back?"* — it's season 2.

Two collections: **(A)** public-domain "found tape" audio for vintage cut-ins woven through episodes, and **(B)** the Substack shelf — articles, media, and sources for the companion evidence locker.

**Rights rule of thumb:** every audio item below notes its public-domain evidence. "AMBER" items are thematically strong but need a rights check before use (their archive.org uploader asserts licensing on the print). When in doubt, do a final clearance pass before release — and never clone a real person's voice.

## Audio Strategy & Sound Design Architecture (from the Gemini thread, 2026-10-10)

*Pasted in by Ash — the three-tier sonic architecture and per-tape cue sheet, built around the verified and AMBER clips above.*

### 1. Sound design & sonic strategy

Three-tier audio architecture so the archival clips sound integral, not tacked on:

- **Tier 1 — High-fidelity presenter/narrative voice.** Clean, dry, modern, hyper-present studio vocal (Sable/host).
- **Tier 2 — The Machine Ledger (archival layer).** Bandpass filtered (high-pass @ 300Hz, low-pass @ 3.5kHz), heavy wow/flutter tape saturation, slight vinyl/cylinder crackle. Cut-ins duck under narration or burst violently through dramatic transitions.
- **Tier 3 — Ambient drone / ritual atmosphere.** Low sub-bass drones, metallic resonant reverb (ziggurat acoustics), static hiss, tape loops.
