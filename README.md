# The Manifesto Tapes

A spoken-word podcast season in development.

Two hosts:

- **Sable Vane** — dry, skeptical archivist of lost futures
- **Cog** — earnest tinker's construct asking naive questions

Driving question: *"Who has been writing the ledger of your life — and what will it cost to take the pen back?"*

- `scripts/` — Field Note scripts, pilot script, casting packet
